🎮 GAME NAME: FireBox – Heart, Mind, Dustbin 

🧠 OBJECTIVE:
Fix and redesign the TapTap game. It must be a decision-based game where the player assigns given Indian family relations into three categories: Heart, Mind, and Dustbin.

---

⚙️ CORE GAMEPLAY:

1. Each round:

* Generate 3 random Indian relations
* Example:
  ["Mother", "Father", "Grandfather"]

2. Player must assign:

* One to ❤️ HEART (most loved)
* One to 🧠 MIND (respect/intelligence)
* One to 🗑️ DUSTBIN (least priority / funny rejection)

3. Player cannot assign same relation to multiple categories

4. After selection:

* Show result screen
* Add humorous/desi reactions

---

👨‍👩‍👧 INDIAN RELATIONS LIST:

Use a large pool:

[
"Mother", "Father",
"Grandmother", "Grandfather",
"Nani", "Nana",
"Chacha", "Chachi",
"Bua", "Fufa",
"Mosi", "Mausa",
"Bhai", "Behen",
"Tau", "Tai"
]

Randomly pick 3 unique relations each round.

---

🎭 REACTION SYSTEM (VERY IMPORTANT):

After player chooses:

Generate funny desi responses:

Examples:

* "Maa ko dustbin me daal diya 😳 kya baat hai!"
* "Dimaag me Papa, dil me Nani ❤️ solid choice!"
* "Chacha ko seedha dustbin 😂 savage!"

Keep responses:

* Light
* Funny
* Non-offensive

---

  * ❤️ HEART
  * 🧠 MIND
