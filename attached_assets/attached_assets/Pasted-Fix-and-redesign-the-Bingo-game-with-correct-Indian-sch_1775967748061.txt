Fix and redesign the Bingo game with correct Indian school-style logic and turn-based number calling.

---

GAME TYPE:
Player vs AI (Turn-based)

---

BOARD SETUP:

* Create TWO separate 5x5 boards:

  1. Player Board (visible)
  2. AI Board (hidden during gameplay)

* Each board:

  * Contains numbers from 1 to 25
  * All numbers must be unique
  * Random arrangement
  * Player must NOT see AI board until game ends

---

TURN-BASED GAMEPLAY:

* Maintain a shared list of numbers from 1 to 25
* Each number can be used only once

---

PLAYER TURN:

* Player selects a number from remaining numbers
* That number is marked on:

  * Player board (if present)
  * AI board (if present)

---

AI TURN:

* AI selects a number from remaining numbers

* That number is marked on:

  * Player board
  * AI board

* Add small delay (300–800ms) before AI move

---

IMPORTANT RULE:

* Same number affects BOTH boards
* This simulates real gameplay where both players cut the same called number

---

LINE COMPLETION RULES:

A line is completed when all 5 cells are marked in:

* Rows (5)
* Columns (5)
* Diagonals (2)

Total possible lines = 12

---

BINGO PROGRESSION:

* 1 line → B

* 2 lines → I

* 3 lines → N

* 4 lines → G

* 5 lines → O

* First to complete 5 lines wins

---

END GAME:

* When game ends:

  * Reveal AI board
  * Show both boards side-by-side
  * Highlight completed lines

---

FUNCTION REQUIREMENTS:

* generateBoard()
* getAvailableNumbers()
* playerMove(number)
* aiMove()
* markNumber(board, number)
* checkCompletedLines(board)
* updateProgress(lines)
* checkWinner()

---

AI LOGIC:

* Easy: random unused number
* Medium: prefer numbers that help AI complete lines

---

UI REQUIREMENTS:

* Show player board clearly
* Hide AI board during game
* Show called numbers history
* Disable already used numbers
* Show B I N G O progress visually

---

RESTRICTIONS:

* No backend
* No APIs
* No libraries
* Pure JavaScript only

---

GOAL:

Accurately recreate the Indian school Bingo experience where both players take turns calling numbers and marking them on their own boards.
