Saanp Wala

Classic Snake Game – Eat, Grow, Survive

▶ Play Now – Free

About the Game

Saanp Wala (Snake Game) is the legendary arcade game that everyone grew up with! Control a snake as it slithers around the board eating food to grow longer. The challenge: avoid hitting the walls or your own ever-growing tail. How long can you survive? Beat your high score with each new game!

How to Play

  • Use arrow keys (or swipe on mobile) to control the snake's direction.
  • Guide the snake to eat the food that appears on screen.
  • Each time you eat, your snake grows one segment longer.
  • Avoid hitting the walls or your own tail – that ends the game.
  • The longer your snake, the higher your score!

Game Features

  • Smooth grid-based snake movement
  • High score tracking
  • Touch / swipe support for mobile play
  • Increasing difficulty as the snake grows
  • Play instantly in browser – no download
