About the Game

Cut-Cross (Tic Tac Toe) is the timeless 3-in-a-row strategy game known all over India. Two players take turns marking X and O on a 3×3 grid. The player who places three of their marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal row wins the round. Simple to learn, endlessly fun!

How to Play

  • Two players take turns – one plays ✕, the other ○.
  • Click any empty cell to place your mark.
  • Get three of your marks in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win.
  • If all 9 cells are filled with no winner, the game is a draw.
  • Play as many rounds as you like!
