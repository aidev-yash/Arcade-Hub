About the Game

Reflex Rush is an exhilarating reaction time test that challenges your speed and focus. Wait for the signal, then tap or click as fast as you can. Track your best times and challenge friends to beat your score. A perfect game to train your reflexes or settle who has the fastest hands!

How to Play

  • Wait for the screen to change colour or show the signal.
  • React as quickly as possible – tap or click immediately!
  • Your reaction time is measured in milliseconds.
  • Avoid tapping too early – that counts as a false start.
  • Play multiple rounds to find your average reaction time.

Game Features

  • Millisecond-accurate reaction time measurement
  • Personal best score tracking
  • Anti-cheat false-start detection
  • Works on mobile and desktop
  • Play instantly in browser – no download
