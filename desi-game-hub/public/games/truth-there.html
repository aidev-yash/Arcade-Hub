Sach ya Jhooth

Truth There – Truth & Dare Party Game

▶ Play Now – Free

About the Game

Sach ya Jhooth (Truth There) is the perfect party game for groups. Players are challenged to answer a truth question honestly or take a dare. With hundreds of fun prompts in both Hindi and English, every round is full of laughs and surprises. Best played with friends and family!

How to Play

  • Players take turns spinning or being selected.
  • Choose "Truth" to answer an honest question or "Dare" to perform a challenge.
  • Refusing a challenge means taking a penalty point!
  • Questions and dares range from funny to spicy – suitable for all ages.
  • Keep playing until everyone has had their turn.

Game Features

  • Hundreds of unique truth questions and dare challenges
  • Hindi & English language support
  • Family-friendly and party modes
  • Works great on mobile during group gatherings
  • Play instantly in browser – no download
