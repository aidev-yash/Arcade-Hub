Bingo – Tambola

Classic Number Lottery Card Game

▶ Play Now – Free

About the Game

Bingo (known as Tambola in India) is a thrilling number-based lottery game. Each player has a 5×5 card filled with random numbers. Numbers are called out one by one, and you mark them on your card. The first player to complete a full row, column, or diagonal shouts "Bingo!" and wins!

How to Play

  • You receive a 5×5 card with unique random numbers (1–75).
  • Numbers are drawn randomly one at a time.
  • Mark each number on your card as it is called.
  • Complete a full row, column, or diagonal to win.
  • First to complete the pattern and call "Bingo!" wins the round.

Game Features

  • Automatic number calling with animations
  • 5×5 Bingo card with unique numbers
  • Win detection for rows, columns, and diagonals
  • Hindi & English language support
  • Play instantly in browser – no download
